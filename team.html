02 · Founders

Where it started.

Seven students from Syosset and Jericho planted Eternal Growth. Meet four of them.

01
DC

Daniel Chen

Founder

Daniel is a violinist and varsity diver from Syosset. As a shortlisted writer for the John Locke Global Essay Competition, a classical violin student at the Manhattan School of Music Precollege Program, and a county-level diver, Daniel is passionate about academics, arts and sports.

02
GH

Gary Han

Founder

Gary Han is a researcher, violist and community leader from Syosset, NY. A 2025 Regeneron ISEF Grand Award winner and YoungArts National Winner in Classical Music, he has discovered new exocomets with deep learning, performed as principal violist at Juilliard Pre-College, and founded nonprofits bringing STEM and music education to underserved students.

03
AZ

Founder

Albert is studying Business Administration at the Tepper School of Business at Carnegie Mellon University, class of 2029. At Syosset High School he was an active member of the DECA club.

AL

Andrew Liu

Founder

Andrew is a freshman at RPI studying aerospace engineering, and likes anything to do with flying or driving. At Syosset High School he helped create Eternal Growth and tutored math, reading and an introduction to computer-aided design. He hopes to work on helicopters or supersonic commercial travel.
