Our officers.

They tutor, and they run the club.

DC

Daniel Chen

President

Daniel is a violinist and varsity diver from Syosset. As a shortlisted writer for the John Locke Global Essay Competition, a classical violin student at the Manhattan School of Music Precollege Program, and a county-level diver, Daniel is passionate about academics, arts and sports.

KF

Katherine Fei

Vice President

Hi! My name is Katherine and I’m a senior from Syosset. I am very passionate about flute, art and business. Eternal Growth has been a long-term commitment for me and I’ve grown a lot throughout my years in it.

JL

Jack Li

Webmaster

Jack Li is a programmer and tutor from Jericho, NY. As an AIME qualifier and USACO Gold division competitor, Jack enjoys passing on his knowledge of math and programming to young students and developing their love of STEM. He maintains the website and helps bring technology into our learning programs.

EB

Eliot Bih

Director of STEM

Hi! My name is Eliot and I’m a senior from Syosset. I’m very passionate about math, science and music!

RL

Robert Liu Meng

Director of Humanities

Hi! I’m Robert. I joined Eternal Growth to give back to the same community that built my strengths. I chose humanities to show the people around me the beauty of finding observations qualitatively, not just quantitatively. I have taken AP Lang, am taking AP Lit, and am exploring philosophy, ideology and beyond.

SN

Sue Nian

Director of Fine Arts

I’ve been playing piano for 13 years and creating art for 6, and both have become essential parts of who I am. I was invited to perform at the annual NYSSMA Piano Festival. I’m also deeply interested in biology and math, and I love exploring how creativity and logic connect in both fields.

VY

Vincent Yang

Director of Sports

As Director of Sports, I encourage students to work and play cooperatively and competitively to keep them active. I have been a classically trained pianist for over ten years and founded Golden Sound, a music group that performs at local senior homes. I tutor younger children, coaching them through skills and confidence.

SM

Sam Moon

Secretary of Membership

Good morning, afternoon and evening! I’m Samuel Moon, a senior at Syosset High School, in charge of the logistics and personnel of our students, so please come to me with any questions. I teach anything from AMC tests, middle and high school math and the SAT to guitar and chess.

IT

Isaac Tsui

Hi! My name is Isaac Tsui and I am the secretary of outreach. I am a junior at Syosset High School, and the things that interest me include history, aviation and literature.

AM

Austin Ma

Treasurer

As Treasurer of Eternal Growth, I guide the selection of organizations that receive our donations, evaluating each to make sure our funds go to causes that have a direct and meaningful impact on our community.

Victor Pan

Treasurer

GH

Gary Han

Founder

Gary Han is a researcher, violist and community leader from Syosset, NY. A 2025 Regeneron ISEF Grand Award winner and YoungArts National Winner in Classical Music, he has discovered new exocomets with deep learning, performed as principal violist at Juilliard Pre-College, and founded nonprofits bringing STEM and music education to underserved students.
