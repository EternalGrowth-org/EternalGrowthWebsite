Who teaches

How it works

  1. First, tell us what you need.

    The form below asks for the subject, the grade and what is getting in the way.

  2. Then we match a tutor.

    Someone who knows the subject well and suits the way your student learns.

  3. Together they make a plan.

    Goals for the term, at a pace that follows what the school is teaching.

  4. Then it grows.

    Regular sessions, steady progress, and a student who starts asking the next question.

Three ways to learn

Every student starts with a tutor of their own. Some stay one-on-one; some join a group; some meet online.

One-on-one

One tutor and one student for the whole session. The best fit for catching up after a hard unit, or racing ahead of one.

Small groups

A few students working through the same unit together. They learn from the tutor and from each other.

Online

Video sessions from anywhere on Long Island, for the weeks when the calendar is already full.

Started by seven students

Gary Han, Daniel Chen, Jack Li, Albert Zhang, Andrew Liu, Leo Dai and Michael Ren founded Eternal Growth to make good tutoring easier to reach for kids across Long Island.

Two founders have since left for Carnegie Mellon and RPI. Today eleven officers run the club, from tutor matching to outreach. What the club raises, our treasurers give to local organizations they choose for their direct effect on the community. Meet the team.
