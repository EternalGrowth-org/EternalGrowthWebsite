From a request to real progress.

  1. Tell us what you need

    The subject, the grade, and what is getting in the way.

  2. We match a tutor

    Someone who knows the subject well and suits the way your student learns.

  3. Together they make a plan

    Goals for the term, at a pace that follows what the school is teaching.

  4. Then it grows

    Regular sessions, steady progress, and a student who starts asking the next question.

Three ways to learn.

One-on-one

One tutor and one student for the whole session. The best fit for catching up, or racing ahead.

Small groups

A few students working through the same unit together, learning from the tutor and from each other.

Online

Video sessions from anywhere on Long Island, for the weeks when the calendar is already full.

Planted by seven students.

Gary Han, Daniel Chen, Jack Li, Albert Zhang, Andrew Liu, Leo Dai and Michael Ren founded Eternal Growth to make good tutoring easier to reach.

Two founders have since left for Carnegie Mellon and RPI. Today eleven officers run the club, from tutor matching to outreach, and what it raises is given to local organizations the treasurers choose.
