ABOUT

A nonprofit dedicated to making education more accessible, run entirely by Long Island high schoolers.

Students teaching studentsLong IslandGiven back

Why we started.

Seven students from Syosset and Jericho founded Eternal Growth to make good tutoring easier to reach.

Gary Han, Daniel Chen, Jack Li, Albert Zhang, Andrew Liu, Leo Dai and Michael Ren set out to do it the most direct way they knew: older students teaching younger ones, in the subjects they know best.

A high schooler remembers what made algebra click, because it happened a few years ago. That memory is the whole method. It shapes how a tutor explains, how patient they are with a wrong turn, and how quickly they notice when something lands.

Our officers are students too. Alongside their own classes they match tutors with families, run outreach and keep the club’s books.

Where the money goes.

What the club raises, we give away to local organizations.

Our treasurers, Austin Ma and Victor Pan, choose which organizations receive our donations. They evaluate each one so that the money goes to causes with a direct and meaningful effect on our community.

What we hold to.

  • Teach it well

    Every student deserves a tutor who knows the subject and takes the time to explain it properly.

  • Open to everyone

    Learning should be within reach of every student, whatever their background or the way they learn.

  • Make it stick

    Games, projects and real examples, because what is enjoyed is remembered.
