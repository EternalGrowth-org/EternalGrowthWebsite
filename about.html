About Eternal Growth

A nonprofit dedicated to increasing the accessibility of education, run by Long Island high school students.

Students teaching students

Seven students from Syosset and Jericho founded Eternal Growth to make good tutoring easier to reach.

Gary Han, Daniel Chen, Jack Li, Albert Zhang, Andrew Liu, Leo Dai and Michael Ren set out to do it in the most direct way they knew: older students teaching younger ones, in the subjects they know best. A high schooler remembers what made algebra click, because it happened a few years ago.

Our officers are students too. Alongside their own classes they match tutors with families, run outreach and keep the club’s books. Meet them.

Where the money goes

What the club raises, we give away to local organizations.

Our treasurers choose which organizations receive our donations. They evaluate each one so that the money goes to causes with a direct and meaningful effect on our community.

What we hold to

  • Teach it well

    Every student deserves a tutor who knows the subject and takes the time to explain it properly.

  • Open to everyone

    Learning should be within reach of every student, whatever their background or the way they learn.

  • Make it stick

    We use games, projects and real examples, because what is enjoyed is remembered.
