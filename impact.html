How the money moves

Raise.

Students raise money through Eternal Growth.

  • Raise. Students raise money through Eternal Growth.
  • Choose. The treasurers decide where it can do the most good.
  • Give. More than $3,500 went to Swim Across America for cancer research.
  • Teach. More helps subsidize tutoring, so more families can take part.
