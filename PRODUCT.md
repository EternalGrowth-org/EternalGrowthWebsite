# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Parents and guardians on Long Island looking for a tutor for a child (elementary through high school), and high school students who might want to tutor with the club. Inferred from the existing site; not separately confirmed.

## Product Purpose
Eternal Growth is a student-run nonprofit. High school students, mostly from Syosset and Jericho, tutor younger kids one-on-one, in small groups and online. Success is a family sending a tutor request through the form.

## Positioning
The tutors are high schoolers only a few years ahead of the students they teach, and they run the whole club themselves. What the club raises is donated to local organizations its treasurers choose.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Seven subject areas: math, English and writing, science, coding, music, social studies, and "beyond the classroom" (study skills, art, chess, college planning).
- Tutor requests go through a Formspree form (`https://formspree.io/f/xqagapza`) with the fields parentName, studentName, email, subject, grade, message. No backend.
- Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript on GitHub Pages at eternalgrowth.org; no build step.
- Pricing and whether tutoring is free are not stated anywhere and must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Eternal Growth. The user asked for a tree theme, a modern and techy look with heavy liquid glass, a Vanta.js animated background and GSAP page transitions, laid out with the restraint of apple.com (user's binding request, 2026-10-24).
- Contact: (347) 878-6615, info@eternalgrowth.org.

## Evidence on Hand
- Officer and founder bios and photos in `images/` (Robert Liu Meng has no photo; Leo Dai and Michael Ren have neither bio nor photo).
- A public-domain-style seedling time-lapse in `assets/seedlings.webm` (CC BY 4.0, credited).
- No testimonials, student counts, grade statistics or reviews exist. Never fabricate them.

## Product Principles
- The tutors are the product: show the real people and what they actually do.
- One clear action everywhere: request a tutor.
- Say only what is true; leave unknowns out rather than filling them.
